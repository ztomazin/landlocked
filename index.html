Landlocked

Be a wealthy billionaire looking to maximize your investment by strategically buying land that prevents the public from accessing public land! There are existing private land purchases that will help you get started. Collaborate with the super wealthy to win! In this scenario, corner-crossing is illegal.
Rules
1. All purple land is counted as private. The goal is to encircle public land with private land. It is considered landlocked if the individual or a group of public land cannot be accessed by other public land.
2. Click on public land to buy it - the darker the green, the more expensive.
3. You can buy two pieces of land per turn or lobby, which is pay money to reduce public backlash. Backlash increases as more public land is made landlocked.
4. Game is over after 15 turns, you cannot buy more land, or backlash hits 100%.
5. When backlash hits 100%, public outcry causes legislation to make corner-crossing legal!
6. When the game is over, your return on investment (ROI) will be calculated. Additionally, how much it would cost to secure the same amount of public land with corner crossing being legal will be displayed.
